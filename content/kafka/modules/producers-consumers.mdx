<Explanation id="intro">

A Kafka partition is an append-only log, and each message in it has a
sequential position called an **offset**. Consumers don't get messages
pushed to them automatically — they PULL, tracking their own position by
periodically **committing** the offset of the last message they've fully
processed. This is what makes consumption resumable: after a crash or
restart, a consumer just reads its last committed offset and continues
from exactly there, rather than losing its place.

```python
def consume_from_offset(log, committed_offset, batch_size):
    batch = log[committed_offset:committed_offset + batch_size]
    new_offset = committed_offset + len(batch)
    return batch, new_offset
```

</Explanation>
